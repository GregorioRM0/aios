# Launch film — what is specific to a product launch

Everything general (contract, numbers, loop, checks) is in the skill. This is only what a launch adds.

## The five files of a launch studio

1. **Rules.** The contract plus launch rules: every product pixel comes from real captures; **never
   redraw UI**; no invented numbers, names or prices; every time lives in one timeline, written in beats,
   that the picture and the sound both read.
2. **Look.** A style card derived from the product's own design system (its `design.md` or equivalent),
   never invented. Without it the model reaches for its defaults: an invented logo, a cream background,
   numbered section labels, a redrawn app.
3. **Kit.** Real screens captured from the product (on synthetic data, never a real workspace: a
   screenshot of a live account leaks names and private content), plus licensed sounds, measured: BPM,
   downbeats, and where each sound actually peaks.
4. **Scenes.** About five, one technique each (a hook, a demo, a morph, a proof, an end card), built in
   parallel against the shared timeline.
5. **Clock.** One timeline joins them; joins are shared shapes, not cuts or fades; the payoff lands on
   the drop.

## The human part

The pain, the payoff and the ending are the operator's call. An agent can draft candidate beats from the
product's features, but the beats are the pitch, and the pitch is theirs. Ask for them.

## Ship

- Export **wide** (16:9, for X / YouTube / a site) and **vertical** (9:16, for Reels / Shorts) from one
  timeline by re-rendering at each size: reframe, never crop.
- Frame one is the thumbnail. Assume no sound. The end card's first and last frames match, so it loops.
- One film per minor release, not per patch.
