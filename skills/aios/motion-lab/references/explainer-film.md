# Explainer film — the `explainer` agent's rung 3

The `explainer` agent's ladder goes text → diagram → interactive HTML → film. A film is rung 3: worth it
only for a public reader who asked for one, or a mechanism that is genuinely about change over time.

## What changes from a general piece

- **GOAL comes from the explainer:** "the reader understands step N of the mechanism", never "the viewer
  is impressed".
- **Silent and captioned.** No narration (an operator's voice is theirs to grant, in INTENT.md). Captions
  are the script: hold each one 0.3 s per word + 0.5 s.
- **SHOTS = the explainer's five-section spine**, one shot per section, each with one hero move that
  shows the change the section describes.
- **Every number on screen is anchored to the source** the explainer cites. A figure that is not in the
  source does not go in the film.
- Output beside the explainer's HTML, same slug: `{slug}.mp4` plus `{slug}.receipts.md`.
