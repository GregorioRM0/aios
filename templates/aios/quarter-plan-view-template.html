      Review

      How to read this view.

      • Committed OKRs are expected to land in full. Aspirational ones are stretch goals ("moonshots"). Doerr, What Matters.
      • Scores run 0.0–1.0, and an objective scores the average of its KRs. Across stretch OKRs, about 0.6–0.7 is the healthy average, and consistently higher means the goals were not ambitious enough. Google re:Work.
      • Weekly confidence comes from the Monday check-in: priorities, forecast, confidence, and health metrics to protect. Wodtke, Radical Focus.
      • Each key result reads baseline → target from a named instrument, so it measures an outcome, not the work. Castro, outputs to outcomes.

      The score here is a draft. The official grade is written once, in the note's Review section, from each instrument read on the review day.
