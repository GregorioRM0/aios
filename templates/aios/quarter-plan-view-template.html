    Review

    How to read this view.

    • Committed OKRs are expected to land in full. Aspirational ones are stretch goals ("moonshots"). Doerr, What Matters.
    • Scores run 0.0–1.0, and an objective scores the average of its KRs. Across stretch OKRs, about 0.6–0.7 is the healthy average (the green arc on each gauge); consistently higher means the goals were not ambitious enough. Google re:Work.
    • Weekly confidence comes from the Monday check-in: priorities, forecast, confidence, and health metrics to protect. Bands: 1–3 off track, 4–6 at risk, 7–10 on track. Wodtke, Radical Focus.
    • Each key result reads baseline → target from a named instrument, so it measures an outcome, not the work. Castro, outputs to outcomes. A hatched amber value means the note marks it to confirm; it is never filled in.

    The score here is a draft. The official grade is written once, in the note's Review section, from each instrument read on the review day.
